Big Tac Toe

How to play
  • The big board has a small tic tac toe board in each of its 9 cells.
  • Players take turns placing X or O in a cell of a small board.
  • The cell you play in decides where your opponent plays next: they must play in the small board at the matching position of the big board.
  • Winning a small board claims that cell of the big board for you.
  • Get three big cells in a row to win the game.
  • If you are sent to a small board that is already won or full, you may play in any open board.
